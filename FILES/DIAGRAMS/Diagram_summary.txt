FLOODSENSE DIAGRAM RECONSTRUCTION AND REVISION GUIDE

Official thesis title:
FLOODSENSE: AN ANDROID-BASED EXPERT SYSTEM FOR FLOOD SUSCEPTIBILITY ASSESSMENT AND PRE-EVENT PREPAREDNESS IN BACOOR CITY

Document purpose:
This plain-text document is the master manual for recreating, checking, and revising every FloodSense diagram in Microsoft Word, Lucidchart, or a comparable diagramming application. It records the exact recommended labels, element placement, connections, data-flow directions, hierarchy, database structure, and restrictions without requiring access to the earlier generated diagram files.

AUTHORITATIVE BASIS AND PRECEDENCE

Use the following sources in this order when a conflict appears:
1. The official title and explicit restrictions stated by the researchers.
2. The latest revised FloodSense Chapter 1.
3. The revised FloodSense concept checklist.
4. The revised detailed feature specification and the later Django-based theoretical-framework/system-architecture document.
5. Approved requirements-gathering questionnaires and validated interview information.
6. Earlier FloodSense diagrams, but only when consistent with items 1 to 5.
7. SURECUT and other senior-thesis materials only for page arrangement, symbol style, caption style, spacing, and visual discipline. Their substantive content must never be copied.

GLOBAL TERMINOLOGY

Use these names consistently in every diagram:
- Resident: the person using the Android application.
- Authorized Administrator: a role-authorized user of the browser-based Web Administration System.
- Approved Institutional Data and Expert Sources: BDRRMO, CPDCO, PAGASA references, and any other source formally approved for the study. This is an external source, not a FloodSense database.
- External Map and Navigation Service: an external provider used for basemap display and optional directions. It is not a FloodSense data store or authority.
- Rule-Based Expert System: the deterministic susceptibility-classification subsystem.
- Decision Support System (DSS): the separate deterministic preparedness-guidance subsystem. It receives an assessment result as read-only context and must never alter the susceptibility class.
- Dynamic GIS Map and Check My Area: the resident-facing map and manually positioned pin workflow.
- Web Administration System: the controlled browser workspace for maintaining, validating, approving, publishing, versioning, rolling back, and auditing authorized content.
- Django REST and GeoDjango Processing Module: the controlled backend/API and spatial-processing layer.
- PostgreSQL/PostGIS Authoritative Database: the central relational and spatial database.

GLOBAL RESTRICTIONS

- FloodSense is scenario-based and pre-event. It is not a real-time forecast, official warning system, road-safety service, live inundation map, live sensor platform, emergency-dispatch system, or autonomous evacuation authority.
- The rainfall intensity and duration chosen by a resident are planning inputs. They are not proof of current rainfall.
- Check My Area uses a manually selected or dragged pin. It does not require or prove the resident's actual GPS location and does not continuously track the resident.
- Susceptibility classes are Low, Moderate, High, and Very High. Uncertain, Insufficient Data, and Outside Supported Area are limitation states, not additional susceptibility classes.
- Public map colors must be produced from the active approved Expert System result. An administrator must not manually paint a polygon to assign a public susceptibility class.
- Only approved and active rules, GIS records, preparedness content, and verified evacuation-center information may be shown publicly.
- External directions do not prove that a road is safe, passable, fastest, or appropriate during an emergency.
- Survey percentages and findings must not be placed in the Fishbone, System Architecture, Context, Use Case, DFD, HIPO, Flowchart, or ERD unless a later approved figure explicitly requires them.
- Do not add Machine Learning, Augmented Reality, Unity, Vuforia, Firebase/Firestore as the primary database, live traffic, crowdsourcing, or a general-purpose AI chatbot.

OPEN CONFIRMATIONS TO KEEP VISIBLE DURING REVISION

- Exact PAGASA numerical thresholds and wording remain subject to adoption of the final verified reference. Diagrams may show the approved category labels but must not invent threshold values.
- The final supported geographic resolution may be barangay, sub-barangay, or expert-defined polygon, depending on the approved evidence.
- Institutional datasets, authorized expert reviewers, rule content, and public-release permissions must be treated as pending until documented.
- Evacuation-center capacity, operating status, contacts, and opening conditions are optional fields and may be displayed only when current and verified.
- The latest revised concept checklist and Chapter 1 include resident accounts and the Django stack. Use them in these diagrams. If a later approved requirements baseline removes accounts, revise the Account and Access portions consistently in every affected diagram.
- Offline cache is optional/implementation-dependent. Do not draw it as an authoritative database unless the research team formally approves it. Any cache must show its date and version.

GLOBAL VISUAL RULES

- Use A4 paper. Use portrait unless a section specifically recommends landscape.
- Keep at least 0.5-inch margins; use 0.6 to 0.75 inch when captions are long.
- Recommended font: Arial. Use 9 to 10 pt inside most shapes, 10 to 11 pt bold for major labels, and 11 to 12 pt for diagram headings.
- Use black or dark-gray connectors, normally 0.75 to 1.25 pt.
- Use elbow connectors when a straight line would cross a shape or another label.
- Keep connector labels horizontal and place each label close to its own line without touching the line.
- Use arrowheads only when direction has meaning. HIPO hierarchy lines and fishbone cause branches do not need arrowheads.
- Keep text centered inside nodes unless the diagram convention requires left-aligned attribute lists.
- Every figure must have a caption below it. Use the thesis's final appendix/figure numbering when inserting it into the manuscript.
- All shapes and connectors should remain native/editable in the chosen diagramming application.

SECTION 1. ISHIKAWA OR FISHBONE DIAGRAMS
======================================================================

Diagram name and type:
FloodSense Ishikawa Fish Bone Diagrams; three cause-and-effect diagrams on one A4 portrait page.

Purpose:
To present three distinct documented problems that FloodSense addresses and to group their contributing causes without using survey percentages or copying the other thesis's transport-related content.

Recommended page size and orientation:
A4, portrait. Place all three fishbones vertically, with equal spacing. Each fishbone occupies approximately one-third of the usable page.

Required symbols, colors, typography, and visual style:
- One thin horizontal spine from a small gray tail triangle on the left to a large gray effect-head triangle on the right.
- Three diagonal cause branches per fishbone: two branches rise above the spine and one falls below it.
- Three small white category rectangles with black borders.
- Plain white background; gray gradient or light-gray fill only for the tail and effect head.
- Arial 7.5 to 9 pt for causes, 8 to 9 pt bold uppercase for category and effect labels.
- No arrowheads are required on cause branches; the conceptual direction is from each cause toward the spine and then toward the effect head.

Recommended overall layout:
- Fishbone 1 at the top, Fishbone 2 at the middle, Fishbone 3 at the bottom.
- The spine runs left to right.
- Category 1 sits upper-left; Category 2 sits upper-right; Category 3 sits below the center.
- Put two short sub-cause lines beside each diagonal branch.
- Put the central problem/effect in bold uppercase inside the large right-facing triangle.

SECTION 6. DATA FLOW DIAGRAMS
======================================================================

DFD family name and type:
FloodSense Data Flow Diagrams; Context Diagram, Level 1, and Level 2 decompositions for Processes 1.0 through 4.0.

Purpose:
To show how information moves between external entities, FloodSense processes, and logical data stores. DFDs do not show database cardinality, implementation classes, screen order, or control-flow Yes/No decisions.

Recommended page size and orientation:
- DFD Level 1: A4 landscape; one complete diagram or four coordinated pages if readability requires.
- DFD Level 2: A4 landscape; use one page per parent-process decomposition.

Required symbols, colors, typography, and visual style:
- External entities: yellow rectangles with dark-gray borders.
- Processes: Gane-Sarson-style rounded boxes with a narrow yellow header and footer, white center, process number at upper-left, and process title centered.
- Data stores: Gane-Sarson stores with a gray identifier panel and yellow name panel.
- Data flows: dark-gray elbow or straight arrows, 1 to 1.25 pt, one arrowhead at destination.
- Off-page connectors: yellow rounded rectangles labeled FROM PROCESS 2.0 or TO PROCESS 3.0 only when a large decomposition is split across pages.
- Arial 9 to 11 pt. Use short noun phrases, not command sentences, for data-flow labels.

Canonical external entities:
E1 Resident
E2 Authorized Administrator
E3 Approved Institutional Data and Expert Sources
E4 External Map and Navigation Service

Canonical logical data stores:
D1 User and Role Records
D2 Rainfall and Spatial Records
D3 Expert Rules and Versions
D4 DSS and Evacuation Content
D5 Sources and Audit Logs

DFD rule:
No external entity may connect directly to a data store. No data store may connect directly to another data store. Every process must have at least one input and one output.

SECTION 8. HIPO DIAGRAMS
======================================================================

Diagram family name and type:
FloodSense Hierarchy plus Input-Process-Output (HIPO) Diagrams; four hierarchy pages.

Purpose:
To show the top-down functional decomposition of FloodSense and identify each module's input, processing responsibility, and output.

Recommended page size and orientation:
A4 landscape, one hierarchy per page.

Required symbols, colors, typography, and visual style:
- White rectangular module boxes with thin black borders and a subtle gray shadow.
- Root module at top center; parent modules in one evenly spaced row; child modules below their correct parent.
- Use thin black orthogonal hierarchy lines with no arrowheads.
- Arial 8 to 9 pt, uppercase in boxes.
- Do not connect sibling modules directly. Every child must connect to its parent trunk.

ERD ENTITY DEFINITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Entity name:
ROLE
Purpose:
Defines resident and authorized administrative role categories.
Primary key:
role_id BIGSERIAL
Foreign keys:
None
Attributes and suggested data types:
role_id BIGSERIAL; code VARCHAR(30); name VARCHAR(80); description TEXT; is_active BOOLEAN; created_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
role_id, code, name, is_active, created_at.
Optional fields:
description.
Unique constraints:
code; name.
Relationships:
Referenced by USER_ACCOUNT.role_id.
Cardinality and optionality:
One ROLE may have zero or many USER_ACCOUNT records; every USER_ACCOUNT must have exactly one ROLE.
Notes:
Minimum controlled codes are RESIDENT and AUTHORIZED_ADMINISTRATOR. Additional roles require documented permission requirements.

Entity name:
USER_ACCOUNT
Purpose:
Stores the minimum approved identity, authentication, role, status, and acknowledgement data for residents and administrators.
Primary key:
user_id UUID
Foreign keys:
role_id BIGINT -> ROLE.role_id
Attributes and suggested data types:
user_id UUID; role_id BIGINT; email CITEXT or VARCHAR(254); password_hash VARCHAR(255); display_name VARCHAR(120); account_status VARCHAR(20); privacy_accepted_at TIMESTAMPTZ; disclaimer_accepted_at TIMESTAMPTZ; last_login_at TIMESTAMPTZ; created_at TIMESTAMPTZ; updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
user_id, role_id, email, password_hash, account_status, created_at, updated_at.
Optional fields:
display_name, privacy_accepted_at until acceptance is required, disclaimer_accepted_at until acknowledgement, last_login_at.
Unique constraints:
Normalized email.
Relationships:
Belongs to ROLE; may have USER_PREFERENCE; may request ASSESSMENT and DSS_SESSION; may create/review/approve versioned records within permission; may create AUDIT_LOG events.
Cardinality and optionality:
Each account has one role; an account may have zero or one preference record and zero or many assessments, DSS sessions, authored records, and audit events.
Notes:
Store only a secure Django password hash. Never store or display a plain-text password.

Entity name:
USER_PREFERENCE
Purpose:
Stores optional resident convenience settings without storing continuous location history.
Primary key:
user_id UUID, also a foreign key
Foreign keys:
user_id UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id; home_area_id BIGINT -> GEOGRAPHIC_AREA.area_id; default_intensity_id BIGINT -> RAINFALL_INTENSITY.intensity_id; default_duration_id BIGINT -> RAINFALL_DURATION.duration_id.
Attributes and suggested data types:
user_id UUID; home_area_id BIGINT; default_intensity_id BIGINT; default_duration_id BIGINT; preferred_map_zoom SMALLINT; updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
user_id, updated_at.
Optional fields:
home_area_id, default_intensity_id, default_duration_id, preferred_map_zoom.
Unique constraints:
user_id.
Relationships:
Optional one-to-one extension of USER_ACCOUNT; optionally references one home area, one default intensity, and one default duration.
Cardinality and optionality:
A USER_ACCOUNT has zero or one USER_PREFERENCE; each preference belongs to exactly one account. Referenced defaults may each be absent.
Notes:
Do not store a Check My Area pin here unless a later approved requirement explicitly authorizes a saved location.

Entity name:
DATA_SOURCE
Purpose:
Records provenance for every institutional dataset, rule, guidance item, map layer, rainfall reference, or center record.
Primary key:
source_id BIGSERIAL
Foreign keys:
created_by UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id
Attributes and suggested data types:
source_id BIGSERIAL; provider_name VARCHAR(160); source_title VARCHAR(255); reference_number VARCHAR(100); source_type VARCHAR(40); date_received DATE; update_date DATE; geographic_coverage TEXT; temporal_coverage TEXT; units TEXT; license_or_terms TEXT; permitted_use TEXT; file_checksum VARCHAR(128); source_status VARCHAR(20); notes TEXT; created_by UUID; created_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
source_id, provider_name, source_title, source_type, source_status, created_by, created_at.
Optional fields:
reference_number, date_received, update_date, geographic_coverage, temporal_coverage, units, license_or_terms, permitted_use, file_checksum, notes.
Unique constraints:
Use a unique constraint on provider_name + source_title + reference_number when a reference number exists; otherwise prevent duplicates through administrative validation.
Relationships:
May be referenced by GIS_LAYER, AREA_FACT, RAINFALL_INTENSITY, RAINFALL_DURATION, EXPERT_RULE, DSS_QUESTION, DSS_GUIDANCE_ITEM, DSS_RULE, and EVACUATION_CENTER.
Cardinality and optionality:
One source may support zero or many controlled records; every public-facing controlled record should reference at least one approved source. If multiple sources are required for one record, add a properly defined source-junction table during implementation.
Notes:
BDRRMO, CPDCO, and PAGASA are provider_name values, not separate database tables.

Entity name:
GIS_LAYER
Purpose:
Defines a versioned approved spatial layer such as a boundary, susceptibility-zone, or evacuation-resource layer.
Primary key:
layer_id BIGSERIAL
Foreign keys:
source_id BIGINT -> DATA_SOURCE.source_id; previous_version_id BIGINT -> GIS_LAYER.layer_id; created_by UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id; reviewed_by UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id; approved_by UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id.
Attributes and suggested data types:
layer_id BIGSERIAL; source_id BIGINT; previous_version_id BIGINT; code VARCHAR(50); name VARCHAR(160); layer_type VARCHAR(40); geometry_type VARCHAR(30); crs_epsg INTEGER; version VARCHAR(30); lifecycle_status VARCHAR(20); effective_from DATE; effective_to DATE; created_by UUID; reviewed_by UUID; approved_by UUID; created_at TIMESTAMPTZ; reviewed_at TIMESTAMPTZ; approved_at TIMESTAMPTZ; published_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
layer_id, source_id, code, name, layer_type, geometry_type, crs_epsg, version, lifecycle_status, created_by, created_at.
Optional fields:
previous_version_id, effective_from, effective_to, reviewed_by, approved_by, reviewed_at, approved_at, published_at.
Unique constraints:
code + version.
Relationships:
Belongs to DATA_SOURCE; may follow a previous GIS_LAYER version; contains GEOGRAPHIC_AREA records.
Cardinality and optionality:
One GIS_LAYER may contain zero or many areas; every GEOGRAPHIC_AREA belongs to exactly one layer.
Notes:
Recommended lifecycle values are DRAFT, VALIDATION, REVIEW, APPROVED, PUBLISHED/ACTIVE, SUPERSEDED, and REJECTED. Use the final approved vocabulary consistently.

Entity name:
GEOGRAPHIC_AREA
Purpose:
Stores a Bacoor barangay, sub-barangay area, or expert-defined supported susceptibility polygon and its hierarchy.
Primary key:
area_id BIGSERIAL
Foreign keys:

Attributes and suggested data types:
area_id BIGSERIAL; layer_id BIGINT; parent_area_id BIGINT; area_code VARCHAR(50); name VARCHAR(160); area_type VARCHAR(40); geometry GEOMETRY(MULTIPOLYGON, 4326); resolution_note TEXT; is_supported BOOLEAN; lifecycle_status VARCHAR(20); version VARCHAR(30); approved_by UUID; approved_at TIMESTAMPTZ; created_at TIMESTAMPTZ; updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
area_id, layer_id, area_code, name, area_type, geometry, is_supported, lifecycle_status, version, created_at, updated_at.
Optional fields:
parent_area_id, resolution_note, approved_by, approved_at.
Unique constraints:
layer_id + area_code; optionally layer_id + name when names are unique within the layer.
Relationships:
Belongs to GIS_LAYER; may have one parent and zero or many child areas; has AREA_FACT records; may scope EXPERT_RULE, ASSESSMENT, DSS_RULE, USER_PREFERENCE, and EVACUATION_CENTER.
Cardinality and optionality:
Each area has zero or one parent; one parent may have zero or many children. Other referencing records each point to zero or one area as specified.
Notes:
The final area_type and precision depend on the approved evidence. Do not label street or property precision unless supported.

Entity name:
AREA_FACT
Purpose:
Stores source-backed geographic or historical attributes used by Expert System rules.
Primary key:
area_fact_id BIGSERIAL
Foreign keys:
area_id BIGINT -> GEOGRAPHIC_AREA.area_id; source_id BIGINT -> DATA_SOURCE.source_id; created_by UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id; approved_by UUID -> USER_ACCOUNT.user_id.
Attributes and suggested data types:
area_fact_id BIGSERIAL; area_id BIGINT; source_id BIGINT; fact_type VARCHAR(60); categorical_value VARCHAR(120); numeric_value NUMERIC(14,4); text_value TEXT; unit VARCHAR(40); valid_from DATE; valid_to DATE; version VARCHAR(30); lifecycle_status VARCHAR(20); created_by UUID; approved_by UUID; created_at TIMESTAMPTZ; approved_at TIMESTAMPTZ.
Required fields:
area_fact_id, area_id, source_id, fact_type, version, lifecycle_status, created_by, created_at.
Optional fields:
categorical_value, numeric_value, text_value, unit, valid_from, valid_to, approved_by, approved_at.
Unique constraints:

SECTION 10. OTHER FORMALLY SPECIFIED INTERNAL PROCESS VIEW
======================================================================

Diagram name and type:
Rule-Based Expert System Inference Sequence; optional technical sub-flow, not a replacement for the required System Flowchart.

Purpose:
To document the exact inference order already specified in the revised requirements for use if the panel requests a separate rule-engine figure.

Recommended page size and orientation:
A4 portrait.

Required symbols and layout:
Use a single top-to-bottom series of process rectangles with two decision diamonds. Keep the same flowchart colors defined in Section 7.

Exact ordered elements:
1. Accept Selected Location, Rainfall Intensity, and Duration
2. Resolve Most Specific Approved Geographic Zone
3. Load Approved Zone Profile and Active Rule-Set Version
4. Retrieve Applicable Active Approved Rules
5. Evaluate Every Required Condition
6. Rank Matches by Geographic Specificity, Condition Specificity, Approval, Priority, and Version
7. Equally Applicable Rules Disagree?
   Yes -> Return Uncertain
   No -> continue
8. Any Applicable Rule?
   No -> Return Insufficient Data
   Yes -> Return Low, Moderate, High, or Very High
9. Generate Explanation, Source, Version, Update Date, and Limitations
10. Pass Valid Result as Read-Only Context to the Separate DSS

Implementation restriction:
Do not show Machine Learning, probabilistic prediction, automatic rule creation, live sensor input, or a DSS-to-Expert-System feedback arrow.
