Verification and resident use

Verified does not mean currently open, reachable, or safe. It does not guarantee space or live capacity.

Verification alone does not make a record eligible for resident output. Eligibility also requires approved publication, an approved publicly releasable source, safe public fields, and a supported geographic identity under the resident API policy.

Boundary association identifies an administrative area; it does not assign flood susceptibility or describe current flood conditions. Approximate straight-line distance is not road distance or a safety recommendation.
