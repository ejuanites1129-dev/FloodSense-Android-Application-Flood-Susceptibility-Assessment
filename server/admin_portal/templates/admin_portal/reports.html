Interpretation

How to read this report

Enabled, approved, published, and verified describe different conditions. These counts do not evaluate whether a resident can receive a record. Source restrictions and existing publication checks still apply.

Administrative boundaries identify areas; they do not establish susceptibility. Scenario references describe hypothetical inputs, not observed rainfall.

Historical trends and time-to-review measures are unavailable in this report. Modification timestamps cannot establish either. No percentages or inferred activity are shown.
